Method and sources

Contracts

All QB contracts from OverTheCap's historical contract archive, distributed by nflverse (pulled October 2026). APY is total value divided by years. A contract counts toward a season if the player was on a roster that season and it was the player's most recently signed deal covering that year, so an extension replaces the old deal from the year it is signed.

Cap hits

Each player's season cap number from the same source. Dead money left behind on a team after a release is not counted. Cap hits are lower than APY in most years because signing bonuses are prorated and contracts are often back-loaded.

Salary cap

Official per-team cap: $62.2M in 2000 up to $301.2M in 2026. 2010 was an uncapped year, so the 2009 cap ($123M) stands in. The league had 31 teams in 2000–01 and 32 from 2002.

Coverage

The archive covers every team's starter from 2012 on and full rosters from about 2013. Before 2012 some teams' QBs are missing (only 14 of 31 teams in 2000), so league-wide totals in the shaded years understate the real share. The top-QB line is the steadier guide there. The 2026 season is in progress, and backup QBs are still being filled in.
